PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains what information this website collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and how you can request access, correction or deletion of information.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Who we are

This is the public website of St. Agnes Educational Centre, a school in Ahero, Kisumu County, Kenya. In this policy, “the school”, “we” and “us” refer to St. Agnes Educational Centre.

You can find our location, phone number and visiting information on our Visit Us page.

Information this website collects

This website is an informational website. It does not have user accounts, logins, newsletter sign-ups, online payments, application forms or contact forms that send data to the school.

The website itself stores the following:

  • Documentation centre entries — if you add an entry on the Visit Us page (document title, document type and description), that entry is saved using your browser’s local storage. It stays on your own device and browser. It is not sent to the school and is not visible to other visitors.
  • Theme preference — if you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the website can remember it.

This website does not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, profiling, fingerprinting or tracking cookies, and it does not build visitor profiles.

When your browser loads font files and the map on the Visit Us page, technical information (such as your IP address) is necessarily exchanged with the providers of those services. These services are listed under Third-party services below.

Why information is collected

  • To make the features you choose to use work — for example, saving a documentation entry so it appears in your list.
  • To remember your display preference (light or dark mode).
  • To serve the website to your browser (standard technical delivery of pages, fonts and the map).

We only ask for what is needed for a feature to work. The website does not request names, dates of birth, home addresses, identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.

How submitted information is used

Documentation entries you create are used for one purpose only: to show the list of entries in your own browser. They are stored locally on your device. They are not emailed to the school, not published to other visitors, and not uploaded to any school system.

If you contact the school by phone or by visiting in person, any information you share with the school directly is handled by the school outside of this website.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

The only third parties involved in serving this website are:

  • Google Fonts — provides the typefaces used on this website.
  • Google Maps — provides the embedded map on the Visit Us page.

Information may also be disclosed where we are required to do so by law.

How long information is retained

  • Documentation entries stay in your browser’s local storage until you delete them (each entry has a delete control) or until you clear this website’s data in your browser settings. The school does not receive them, so the school does not retain them.
  • Your theme preference is kept until you clear this website’s data in your browser.
  • The website does not keep a database of visitor submissions.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies. There are no advertising cookies, analytics cookies or social media cookies, so no cookie consent banner is shown.

The website uses your browser’s local storage, which works in a similar way to a small local file, to store:

  • st-agnes-theme — your light/dark display preference.
  • stAgnesDocuments — documentation entries you create on the Visit Us page.

You are in control of this data. To remove it at any time, use your browser’s “clear site data” option for this website, or delete individual entries in the documentation centre.

Children’s privacy

This is a school website, so we treat children’s information with particular care.

  • The website does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • There are no forms on this website directed at children, and no feature asks children for personal details.
  • Student names, contact details, identification numbers, academic records and home addresses are not published on this website.
  • Photographs of students will only be published with the school’s authorisation, and will not be labelled with personal details unless the school has approved that information for publication.
  • If a future feature would collect information from children, it will be clearly flagged for school and legal review before it goes live, and this policy will be updated.

Third-party services

This website loads the following external services:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) — delivers the Fraunces and Inter typefaces used for the website’s text. Both typefaces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. When your browser requests these files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — an embedded map on the Visit Us page so visitors can find the school. The map is loaded directly from Google, which may process technical information and use cookies according to its own policies. The “Open Maps” button on the Visit Us page opens Google Maps in a new tab.

The website has no analytics scripts, advertising networks, tag managers, chat widgets, CAPTCHA services or social media plugins.

Accessing, correcting or deleting your information

To request deletion or correction of information submitted through this website, contact the school using the contact details provided on the Visit Us page.

Documentation entries and your theme preference are stored only in your own browser, so you can also remove them yourself at any time by deleting individual entries or by clearing this website’s data in your browser settings.
